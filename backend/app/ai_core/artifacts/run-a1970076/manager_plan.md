### Brief Translation & Initial Assessment

The client's brief is: "Кебабы рядом со школой. Ркебабы кототрые будут для веганов рядом со школами на машинках передвиждые."

**Translation:** "Kebabs near the school. Kebabs that will be for vegans near schools on mobile carts."

**Initial Assessment:** This brief provides a core product idea (vegan kebabs), a target location (near schools), and a distribution method (mobile carts). However, it is extremely high-level and lacks critical marketing details required to develop a comprehensive campaign.

### Artifact Checklist & Regeneration Request

Based on our standard checklist, the following essential artifacts are missing or incomplete:

1.  **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):**
    *   **Missing:** We need a detailed understanding of who our target customers are. "Students near schools" is too broad. Are we targeting high school students, college students, parents picking up kids, school staff? What are their dietary habits, price sensitivity, motivations for choosing vegan options, and media consumption habits?
    *   **Why it's crucial:** Without a clear ICP, we cannot tailor our messaging, choose effective marketing channels, or define the brand's appeal.
    *   **Regeneration Request:** Please provide a detailed ICP, including demographics, psychographics, needs, pain points, and daily routines of the primary target audience.

2.  **Concepts:**
    *   **Missing:** We have a product idea, but no marketing concepts. What is the brand's name? What is its unique selling proposition (USP) beyond "vegan kebabs"? What is the desired brand personality, visual identity, and overall tone of voice? What kind of experience are we trying to create?
    *   **Why it's crucial:** Concepts define the brand's identity and how it will differentiate itself in the market. They guide all creative development.
    *   **Regeneration Request:** Please provide initial brand concepts, including potential brand names, a clear USP, desired brand personality (e.g., fun, healthy, edgy), and any initial thoughts on visual style or key messages.

3.  **Texts:**
    *   **Missing:** There are no marketing texts provided. We need examples of slogans, ad copy, social media post ideas, or even a basic menu description to understand the desired messaging style and content.
    *   **Why it's crucial:** Texts are the direct communication with our audience. They need to align with the ICP and concepts to be effective.
    *   **Regeneration Request:** Please provide examples of desired marketing texts, such as potential slogans, a short paragraph describing the product's appeal, or ideas for social media posts.
