    *   **Value:** Getting quality service at a fair price.

*   **Problems/Barriers:**
    *   **Low Awareness:** Potential customers may not know about the combined service or its benefits.
    *   **Perceived Niche:** Some might see tire inflation as a separate, infrequent task, not easily combined with a wash.
    *   **Competition:** Existing standalone car washes or gas stations offering basic air.
    *   **Location Dependency:** Success heavily relies on convenient access and visibility.
    *   **Trust & Quality:** Building trust in both services (accurate tire pressure, effective wash).

    *   **"Small Luxuries":** A clean car provides a sense of well-being and pride, even for those on a budget.
    *   **"Hidden Value":** Educating customers on the benefits of proper tire pressure (fuel economy, safety, tire life) can elevate the perceived value of the inflation service beyond just "free air."
